CalR
Why. Analysis of indirect calorimetry experiments requires specialized statistical treatment to account for differential contributions of fat mass and lean mass to metabolic rates.
What. CalR is designed to help scientists translate these guidelines into practice and to provide standardized methods for reproducible research.
How. CalR imports data files from instruments designed by Columbus Instruments, TSE, and Sable, automatically determines the appropriate statistical tests, performs basic statistical analyses, and generates graphical output.
